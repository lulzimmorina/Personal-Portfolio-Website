My Vision

The Path to Proficiency: Finishing what I start.

"I’ll be honest: Claiming that I always finish what I begin would be the biggest lie I’ve ever told. Like many of us, I’ve left trails of half-finished ideas behind me. But that stops here.
This website is my commitment to change. It’s built on the belief that everything starts with a single step, followed by another, and another. I’ve come to realize that in the pursuit of mastery, you never truly 'arrive'. The journey itself is the destination. It’s about how you walk the path.

This is my Path to Proficiency.

My goal is simple: to take the small skills I possess, refine them, and master them. I’m doing this to prove to the most important person in my life that I can see things through:

To prove it to myself!"

About Me

20 Years of Mats, 0 Miles of Moving, and One New Path.

"For nearly two decades, my world was defined by the dimensions of a Karate dojo. If there is one thing martial arts taught me, it’s that mastery isn't about the black belt—it's about showing up for the thousandth basic punch when no one is watching.

I’ve spent my life in the same place, but my mind has been everywhere. I’ve started degrees, explored different industries, and 'sniffed' into various careers. For a long time, I felt like I was just collecting unfinished chapters.

But now, I’m changing the narrative.

I’ve traded the Gi for a keyboard. HTML and CSS are my new 'Katas.' I’m building this site not just to learn code, but to prove that I can take all those scattered experiences and channel them into one finished product.

I might call myself a 'birdbrain' when JavaScript gets tough, but if I can stand on a mat for 20 years, I can certainly figure out a 'div'. I'm not just looking for a job; I'm looking for the place where my persistence finally meets its purpose."
